RTIH Outposts

Extending Andhra Pradesh’s Innovation Network Across Regions

RTIH Outposts are institutional partners within the RTIH statewide innovation network, enabling universities, colleges, and regional centres to deliver startup support locally.

What RTIH Outposts Are

RTIH is a Network — Not a Single Hub

RTIH operates as a networked innovation ecosystem, with central coordination and distributed Outposts working together to enable inclusive, region-led entrepreneurship across Andhra Pradesh.

Outpost Types

Level 1

Anchor Institutions

Established universities and mature incubators that act as regional anchors, incubating startups and supporting emerging Outposts across the ecosystem.

Level 2

Regional & Emerging Centres

Colleges, EDCs, and town or district-level centres that provide accessible local startup support and build strong regional innovation pipelines.
