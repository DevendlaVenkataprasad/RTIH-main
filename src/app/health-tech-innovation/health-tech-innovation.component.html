About the Challenge

This Hackathon / EoI extends the Government of Andhra Pradesh's initiative to identify and demonstrate portable, affordable and scalable medical technologies that can be deployed in community health centres, mobile units and rural settings. We seek solutions that improve diagnostics, patient monitoring and telemedicine capabilities while being low-cost and easy to operate.

Problem Statement

Transforming Public Healthcare Through Innovation
Andhra Pradesh aims to transform public healthcare delivery by adopting next-generation medical technologies that are portable, low-cost, and impactful. The challenge is to bring forward solutions that can meaningfully bridge healthcare gaps—especially in rural areas—through faster diagnosis, better monitoring, and improved patient care. Innovations should be practical, scalable, and ready for real-world deployment.

Eligibility & Participation

  • Legally registered organizations, startups, manufacturers, research institutions.
  • Preferably minimum 3 years of domain experience or demonstrable competency in medical technologies.
  • Compliance with applicable regulatory standards (CDSCO, ISO 13485, BIS, ICMR, FDA where relevant).
  • Willingness to participate in live demonstration and pilot implementation in Andhra Pradesh.

What to Prepare

Organization profile, technology overview, TRL (Technology Readiness Level), regulatory certificates, deployment plan, and a short demo video or presentation.
