A Global Network for Building Andhra Pradesh’s Innovation Future

Sunrise Connects brings global Telugu expertise into a structured platform for mentorship, investment, collaboration, skilling, and market access.

Purpose

Sunrise Connects is a global initiative by RTIH that brings together entrepreneurs, professionals, investors, mentors, researchers, corporates, and changemakers of Telugu origin to collectively strengthen Andhra Pradesh’s innovation and startup ecosystem.

Impact

It connects global experience with local ambition, enabling meaningful contributions toward entrepreneurship, innovation, skill development, and economic transformation.

Global Expertise Mentors, investors, researchers, operators and professionals
Local Innovation Students, founders, incubators and ecosystem builders across Andhra Pradesh
Structured Access Capital, markets, partnerships, knowledge and opportunity pathways
Andhra Pradesh Has Talent

What It Needs Is Stronger Global Connectivity

Across the world, Telugu professionals and entrepreneurs are building companies, leading research, investing in innovation, and shaping industries.

At the same time, thousands of students, founders, innovators, and aspiring entrepreneurs across Andhra Pradesh are looking for access to mentorship, networks, markets, capital, knowledge, and opportunity.

Sunrise Connects exists to bridge these worlds. Not as a one-time initiative.

But as a long-term movement to build a globally connected innovation ecosystem for Andhra Pradesh.

A Platform for Contribution

Sunrise Connects gives every member a clear way to contribute through mentorship, investment, collaboration, access, and global advocacy.
