About InnoTribe

InnoTribe is a structured campus-to-incubation pathway that helps students:

  • Identify real-world problems
  • Build startup capabilities
  • Form venture teams
  • Validate and launch ideas
person_add Open Entry

Students from any discipline can begin with awareness, problem discovery, and orientation.
