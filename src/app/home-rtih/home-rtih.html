What is RTIH?

Ratan Tata Innovation Hub is Andhra Pradesh's flagship initiative to transform the state into a global startup hub. Operating on a Hub-and-Spoke model, RTIH at Amaravati acts as the central hub, supported by five regional centers across Visakhapatnam, Vijayawada, Rajahmundry, Tirupati, and Anantapur.

Ratan Tata Innovation Hub Designed to

  • Nurture innovation and entrepreneurship across the state
  • Connect startups with markets, research, and government initiatives
  • Drive cutting-edge innovation in AI, biotech, clean energy, advanced manufacturing, and emerging technologies
  • Promote inclusivity by supporting women, rural innovators, and underrepresented groups
