Ratan Tata Innovation Hub, an initiative under Andhra Pradesh’s Innovation & Startup Policy 2024–2029, is the state’s central platform for innovation, incubation and entrepreneurship. With a hub in Amaravati and spokes in Vijayawada, Anantapur, Visakhapatnam, Tirupati and Rajahmundry RTIH fosters an, inclusive startup ecosystem. It offers expert mentorship, funding support and cutting-edge infrastructure while uniting government, academia and industry. By 2029, RTIH aims to enable 20,000 startups, create 1,00,000 jobs and establish 10 Centers of Excellence across Andhra Pradesh.

Vision

To position Andhra Pradesh as a Global Startup Hub for Innovation, Incubation, and Entrepreneurship driving inclusive economic growth.

Mission

  • Support 20,000 startups and create 1,00,000 jobs by 2029
  • Enable 20 Soonicorns and 10 Unicorns from Andhra Pradesh
  • Build 10 Centers of Excellence in emerging technologies
  • Foster grassroots innovation across Tier-2 & Tier-3 cities
  • Vijayawada
  • Visakhapatnam
  • Rajamahendravaram
  • Tirupati
  • Ananthapuramu
